About Monogra

An independent guide for people who want a bag to last twenty years.

Monogra began with a repaired bag and an argument about whether it had been worth repairing. It had. The point of this site is that most people cannot tell which bags are worth it, because the information is buried under marketing.

What we publish

Two long guides and three free tools. The Leather Care Guide covers cleaning, conditioning, storage and repair. The Bag Buying Guide covers construction, hardware, sizing and second-hand buying. The tools answer the three questions we were asked most while writing them.

How we stay independent

We take no payment, gifts or samples in exchange for naming a brand, and there are no shopping or affiliate links anywhere on this site. We deliberately write about construction rather than labels. If ads appear, a third party serves them and they have no influence on what we publish.

What we will not do

We do not authenticate bags, and we will not try to over email or chat. Counterfeits are good enough now that photograph-based judgements are unreliable, and a wrong answer in either direction can cost somebody a great deal. Authentication belongs with the brand or an accredited specialist.

How we work

Our guidance comes from leather and tanning reference material, published care standards from manufacturers, repairers we have worked with and ordinary long-term use. Where the right answer depends on the finish or the maker, we say so. Our Editorial Policy explains how we research, review and correct what we publish.

Corrections
